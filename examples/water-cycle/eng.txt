The sun heats up the water.
Water turns into invisible gas.
This gas is called water vapor.
The vapor rises into the sky.
Cool air causes the vapor to change.
It changes back into tiny droplets.
These droplets form clouds.
The clouds are made of water.
The water droplets get heavier.
Eventually, the water falls to Earth.
This falling water is called rain.
Sometimes, the water falls as snow.
It can also fall as sleet or hail.
When it hits the ground, it pools.
This pooling is called collection.
Some water soaks into the ground.
This is called infiltration.
Water flowing over land is runoff.
The water returns to rivers.
Rivers flow into larger bodies of water.
These bodies include lakes and oceans.
Plants also absorb water from the soil.
This process is called transpiration.
The cycle repeats constantly.
Water cycles endlessly around the planet.
